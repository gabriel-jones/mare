# Implementation plan

Phase A implemented: solo playable foundation — isometric WASD, seeded terrain, grid blueprints, material spending, manual construction, solar/battery lighting, enterable fixed-footprint habitat, save/load.

Acceptance: move and jump, place/rotate/cancel valid footprints, reject invalid/overlapping placements, hold E to construct in range, observe night lights and battery accounting, reload saved world. Keep art viewer intact.

Phase B implemented: mining and production — the starting mining rover, designated mining areas with finite regolith, hoppers, conveyor belts with designated machine ports, a regolith processor (metal, silicon, oxygen), storage crates the player collects from, and dismantling.

Acceptance: designate a mining area; the rover paths around structures, digs, depletes cells, returns to the nearest hopper, unloads and recharges; the hopper feeds a belt only from its output port; belts keep spacing, chain, queue when blocked and refuse belts pointing back; the processor accepts regolith only at its input port, stops without power and when its output is blocked; refined goods reach a crate and E collects them; saves round-trip mid-production and version 1 colonies migrate with a starter rover.

Phase C implemented: rooms, food and life support — rooms painted square by square (habitat, greenhouse) joining into one building, airlock squares as the only way in, pressurisation from the oxygen reserve with power and a clear airlock, grow beds, a galley, a water recycler with a starting water stopgap (ice arrives in D), hunger, and a ten-slot carried inventory.

Acceptance: drag-paint squares that join habitats and greenhouses with no wall between them; airlocks must back onto a room and open onto clear ground, and nothing solid or roofed may cover an airlock entrance; a room cannot be finished around the player until it has a way out; rooms fill from the oxygen reserve and pressurise only when powered with a clear airlock; grow beds (greenhouse only) drink water and power to ripen potatoes that E harvests; the galley (habitat only) cooks two potatoes into a meal; the recycler reclaims water; satiety drains over ten minutes, slows walking and building when low, never kills, and raw potatoes or meals restore it; carried goods stack in ten slots and full pockets refuse refunds; version 2 colonies migrate habitat modules to painted squares with an airlock.

Phase D implemented: the crater expedition — a ship AI objective panel that walks a new colony through power, shelter and mining, then sends the player to a derelict robotic lander in a crater east of the base. The processor is locked until its core is salvaged (hold E at the lander) and installed (E at the Starship). The crater's shadowed floor holds water ice that the rover mines into a hopper; belts carry it to a new ice melter that fills the water reserve. Unsettling details only: three-toed tracks leading from the lander over the rim, pried-open panels, and a figure on the rim that slips away when approached or looked toward.

Acceptance: a new colony's objectives advance in order (and skip ahead when work is already done) with a waypoint beam and a direction marker; the processor shows as locked with the reason and refuses placement; holding E for three seconds at the lander yields the core (refused with full pockets) and E at the ship installs it and unlocks processors; a mining area touching ice mines only ice, the rover never mixes loads, and a belted, powered melter turns ice into water up to the cap; the rim figure appears only off the player's line of sight, never acts and vanishes; saves round-trip the mission at version 4, and older colonies count as already repaired.

Local play: http://127.0.0.1:8765/game/ (`?sandbox=1` plays a throwaway colony on a separate save). Controls: WASD, Space, letters select build tools (P solar, B battery, L light, O hopper, C conveyor, X processor, I ice melter, V crate, M mining area, H habitat, G greenhouse, Q airlock, T grow bed, K galley, Y recycler, Z dismantle; press again to deselect), 1–9 and 0 select inventory slots, F eats (the selected food, else the best food carried), R rotates, click places, drag paints room squares or lays a belt route that can turn corners (and redirects belts it crosses), Escape dismisses selection, E held builds within 5m and pressed beside a ripe grow bed, galley or crate uses it, held at the crater lander salvages its core and pressed at the Starship installs it; the Dismantle tool (Build → Tools, or Z) removes whatever is clicked or dragged over, fittings before floors, for a full refund, with a red highlight and the reason when something can't come down yet; Delete or Backspace does the same for the structure you're pointing at. Scroll zooms. Autosave every 20s and on page exit. Time×12 accelerates power/daylight/production/life support, not movement.

Verification: 54 automated simulation/controller/production/habitat/life-support/export checks pass, plus the real backed-up v2 save migrates and round-trips. Browser-verified (sandbox and isolated preview): drag painting with edge-connected strokes, greenhouse glazing and joined roof cut-away, airlock entry, eating, harvest, galley cooking and collection, hotbar and build palette. No multiplayer or full colony-load benchmarks yet.

Next: D crater mission and caves (including the damaged Starship processor story beat — Phase B's processor is buildable without it for now); E satellite launch/research; F co-op (command boundaries start in A, integration after B); G alien retaliation; H outposts/scale/Mars.

Current prototype day length, recipes, belt speeds, rover capacity, storage sizes, hunger rate, crop and cooking times are temporary tuning values. Realistic fuel carbon source remains unresolved. Orbital/launch gameplay arrives with E.
